# New Categories Added: Email & Office

## Summary

Two new categories have been added to Smart Tab Groups:

### 1. Communication (Email) - 🔴 Red Color
Automatically groups all email and communication services.

**Supported Domains:**
- Gmail (gmail.com, mail.google.com)
- Outlook (outlook.com, outlook.office.com, outlook.office365.com)
- Hotmail (hotmail.com)
- Yahoo Mail (yahoo.com, mail.yahoo.com)
- ProtonMail (protonmail.com)
- Tutanota (tutanota.com)
- iCloud Mail (icloud.com)
- AOL Mail (aol.com)
- Zoho Mail (zoho.com)
- Mailchimp, SendGrid, SparkPost (email marketing services)

### 2. Work (Office & Productivity) - 🔵 Blue Color
Automatically groups office suites, cloud storage, project management, and collaboration tools.

**Google Workspace:**
- Google Docs (docs.google.com)
- Google Sheets (sheets.google.com)
- Google Slides (slides.google.com)
- Google Drive (drive.google.com)
- Google Calendar (calendar.google.com)
- Google Keep (keep.google.com)
- Google Tasks (tasks.google.com)
- Google Classroom (classroom.google.com)
- Google Meet (meet.google.com)
- Google Forms (forms.google.com)

**Microsoft 365:**
- Office Online (office.com, office365.com)
- OneDrive (onedrive.com, onedrive.live.com)
- SharePoint (sharepoint.com)
- Microsoft Teams (teams.microsoft.com)
- Power BI, Power Automate, Power Apps

**Cloud Platforms:**
- Azure (portal.azure.com, azure.microsoft.com)
- AWS (aws.amazon.com, console.aws.amazon.com)
- Google Cloud (cloud.google.com, firebase.google.com)
- Heroku, Vercel, Netlify

**Project Management & Collaboration:**
- Trello, Asana, Monday.com
- Notion (notion.so, notion.site)
- Airtable
- Slack, Zoom, Webex
- Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket (Atlassian)
- Basecamp, ClickUp, Linear
- Figma, Canva, Miro, Lucidchart
- Dropbox, Box

## How to Use

1. **Reload the extension** at `chrome://extensions/` (click the reload button)
2. **Open tabs** from email or office services
3. **Click the extension icon** to see proposed groups
4. **Click "Apply Groups"** to create the tab groups

## Example

Open these tabs to test:
- https://gmail.com
- https://outlook.com
- https://docs.google.com
- https://office.com
- https://notion.so
- https://slack.com
- https://zoom.us

You should see:
- 🔴 **Communication** (2 tabs): Gmail, Outlook
- 🔵 **Work** (4+ tabs): Google Docs, Office, Notion, Slack, Zoom

## Customization

You can add more email or office domains in the Settings page:
1. Click extension icon
2. Click "Edit Rules"
3. Add custom domain rules for "Communication" or "Work"

## Color Notes

- **Communication** uses RED color - distinct and urgent
- **Work** shares BLUE color with Development (both are productive work categories)
